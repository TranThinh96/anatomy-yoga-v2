The 3D bone meshes in this folder (skeleton.bin) and the anatomical data in
src/data/body-model.gen.js (joint centres, muscle attachment points and paths,
floor-contact points, segment landmarks) are derived from BodyParts3D
(via the STL conversion at https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D).

"BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under
CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan"
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/deed.en

Modifications (tools/bp3d-build.mjs): converted to metres and to the app's axes,
recentred (midline x = 0, floor y = 0), upper limbs rotated about the shoulder centre
to hang vertically, right side replaced by the mirror image of the left, bone meshes
simplified by vertex clustering and quantised to 16 bit; joint centres, muscle paths
and landmarks computed from the bone and muscle surfaces.
These derived works are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 2.1 JP licence.
