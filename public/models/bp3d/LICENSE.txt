The 3D bone meshes in this folder are derived from BodyParts3D
(FMA16586, FMA16587, FMA16202, FMA24474, FMA24475, FMA24486, FMA24487; via the STL
conversion at https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D).

"BodyParts3D, © The Database Center for Life Science licensed under
CC Attribution-Share Alike 2.1 Japan"
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.1/jp/deed.en

Modifications: converted to metres and to the app's axes, uniformly scaled and
translated to the app's skeleton, femora rotated about the femoral head centre to the
skeleton's thigh axis, simplified and quantised (tools/bp3d-import.mjs).
These derived meshes are distributed under the same CC BY-SA 2.1 JP licence.
